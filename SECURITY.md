# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Security fixes are made on the latest minor release. After a new major version, the previous major gets security fixes for six months.

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| latest `1.x` | ✅ |
| `0.x` | ❌ |

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities in public issues, discussions or pull requests.**

Report them privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Unfield/Tern/security/advisories/new). Include:

- A description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The affected version(s)
- Any suggested fix, if you have one

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We will keep you updated as we investigate and work on a fix.
- Once a fix is released, we will publish an advisory and credit you, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

Thank you for helping keep Tern and its users safe.
